Our Team

Built by Advisors Who've Seen It All

Three Trees was built by two advisors who saw firsthand how retirees were failed by vague, accumulation-focused advice. We built the practice we wished existed.

Michael Cooley

Co-Founder & Lead Advisor — Retirement Income Specialist

ChFC® RICP® NSSA® M.Ed.

With over 20 years in financial services, Michael has dedicated the last decade exclusively to retirement income planning. He developed the Three Trees Framework after watching too many clients reach retirement without a clear income plan. His specialty is helping high-net-worth pre-retirees transition from accumulation to distribution mode with confidence and clarity. He holds the Chartered Financial Consultant (ChFC®), Retirement Income Certified Professional (RICP®), and National Social Security Advisor (NSSA®) designations, along with a Master of Education degree.

Michael believes that the biggest financial risk in retirement isn't a bad market — it's a bad plan. His mission is to ensure every client has a system they understand, trust, and can rely on for decades.

Stephanie Cooley

Co-Founder & Client Experience Director — Tax & Retirement Planning Specialist

ChFC® RICP® Tax Specialist

Stephanie leads client relationships and the tax planning side of the Three Trees process. Her background in tax strategy and estate planning means clients receive a truly comprehensive plan — not just investment management. She is passionate about helping couples align on their retirement vision and navigate the emotional transition from saving to spending.

Stephanie's approach is deeply personal. She takes the time to understand each client's full picture — their fears, their goals, their family dynamics — and ensures the plan reflects who they are, not just what they have saved. Partners in life and in practice, built to serve your retirement with integrity.
