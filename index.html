The Framework

The Three Trees

T R E E   O N E

Now Tree

Year 1 + Emergency Fund

CASH & MONEY MARKETS

Your income today. Completely protected.

Immediate liquidity. Completely immune to market volatility. Your income for today is funded and protected without touching any long-term assets. A market crash tomorrow does not change your income by a single dollar.

WHAT IT HOLDS

Cash, money market funds, short-term CDs, and T-bills. Zero market exposure.

WHAT IT PROTECTS AGAINST

Sequence-of-returns risk. You never have to sell a depressed asset to pay your bills.

T R E E   T W O

Soon Tree

Years 2–10

FIXED INCOME

A decade of dependable income.

Protects your principal from market downturns while delivering steady, predictable cash flow. The Soon Tree automatically refills the Now Tree each year.

WHAT IT HOLDS

Fixed income instruments: bonds, bond ladders, fixed annuities, CDs, and structured income products.

WHAT IT PROTECTS AGAINST

Intermediate-term market volatility. Principal is shielded while income flows consistently.

T R E E   T H R E E

Later Tree

Years 11–40+

TACTICAL GROWTH

Your legacy. Fully unleashed.

Long-term growth without panic. The Later Tree has 10+ years before you need to touch it — meaning it can fully participate in market upside while the other trees fund your income.

WHAT IT HOLDS

Diversified equity portfolios, growth-oriented mutual funds, ETFs, and alternative investments.

WHAT IT PROTECTS AGAINST

Inflation and longevity risk. Your purchasing power grows over decades to sustain 30–40 years.

Beyond the Trees

The Triple Shield Framework

The Three Trees handle your income structure. The Triple Shield handles the three greatest threats to your retirement security.

Most retirees know about market risk — but fewer understand how tax risk and spending risk silently erode decades of accumulated wealth. Our Triple Shield addresses all three simultaneously.

"The greatest destroyer of retirement wealth isn't a market crash. It's the slow, invisible erosion from taxes and unchecked spending."

Three Shields. One Retirement.

Comprehensive protection against the risks that matter most

Shield One

Market Risk

Sequence risk — a major drop early in retirement can permanently derail your plan.

The sequence of returns determines retirement success. A 30% market drop in Year 1 is catastrophic if you're selling assets to live. Our Three Trees structure ensures you never sell a depressed asset to fund your lifestyle.

  • Year 1 income is 100% liquid and unaffected by markets
  • 10+ years of income shielded from equity volatility
  • Later Tree has full time horizon to recover from corrections

Shield Two

Tax Risk

Taxes can quietly erode your savings — over 30 years this compounds dramatically.

Tax laws change. RMD rules change. Your tax bracket in retirement may be higher than you think. We use strategic Roth conversion planning and tax-bracket management to minimize your total lifetime tax burden.

  • Roth conversion laddering before RMDs begin
  • Tax-diversified withdrawal sequencing
  • Proactive bracket management every year

Shield Three

Spending Risk

Outliving your money is a real risk — but so is spending too little and sacrificing your retirement.

Guardrail-based spending strategies protect against both over-spending and under-spending. We establish upper and lower guardrails so you know exactly when and how to adjust — giving you confidence without unnecessary sacrifice.

  • Dynamic withdrawal guardrails, not static percentages
  • Pre-defined triggers for spending adjustments
  • Sustainable income for 30–40 year retirements

Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ

How old is your firm? How long have you been around?

Three Trees Retirement Partners was founded by Michael and Stephanie Cooley after decades of combined experience in the financial services industry. While the firm itself is newer, the experience, credentials, and philosophy behind it are the result of over 20 years of working with retirees. We built this firm specifically to solve the retirement income problem the right way — and we bring all of that experience to every client we serve.

Are you a fiduciary? Do you sell products?

Yes — we are fiduciaries, always. That means we are legally required to put your interests first, not ours. We operate as fee-only advisors and do not earn commissions from product sales. Our only incentive is your financial success. When we recommend a strategy, you can trust it's because it's right for you — not because we get paid more for it.

What makes you different from other financial advisors?

Most advisors focus on accumulation — growing your portfolio as large as possible. We specialize exclusively in retirement income distribution. We don't just manage investments; we build comprehensive income systems that address market risk, tax risk, and spending risk simultaneously. The Three Trees Framework is a proprietary, structured approach that gives you predictability and protection most advisors don't offer. We also happen to be a husband-and-wife team, which means our clients get two perspectives and the benefit of advisors who truly understand the relational side of retirement planning.

Who do you typically work with?

We work best with pre-retirees and retirees who have investable assets of $500,000 or more and are within 5 years of retirement or recently retired. Our clients are typically people who have done the hard work of saving but now need a clear, structured plan for generating reliable income from those savings. They value expertise, appreciate a systematic approach, and want to feel truly confident about their financial future — not just hopeful.

Do I have to leave my current advisor to work with you?

Not necessarily. We are happy to do a second-opinion review of your current plan to identify gaps, risks, and opportunities. Many clients come to us for a retirement income plan while keeping other relationships in place. That said, most clients who engage us for a full plan choose to consolidate — because having one integrated strategy is far more effective than having multiple advisors working in silos. We'll be honest about what we think is best for your specific situation.

When should I claim Social Security?

This is one of the most consequential decisions you'll make in retirement — and the answer is different for everyone. The right claiming age depends on your health, your spouse's benefit, your other income sources, your tax situation, and your overall retirement plan. Claiming too early can cost you hundreds of thousands of dollars in lifetime income. Waiting too long may not make sense given your health or cash flow needs. We run a complete Social Security optimization analysis for every client to determine the ideal claiming strategy for your specific situation. Michael holds the National Social Security Advisor (NSSA®) designation specifically to help clients navigate this decision.

Do you help with tax strategy in retirement?

Absolutely — and this is one of the areas where we add the most value. Tax planning in retirement is dramatically different from tax planning during your working years. We focus on Roth conversion laddering, withdrawal sequencing, required minimum distribution (RMD) planning, and bracket management to minimize your total lifetime tax burden. We coordinate closely with your CPA or tax preparer, or can help you find the right tax professional if you need one. Stephanie specializes in the tax-planning side of our work and ensures every client has a multi-year tax strategy — not just a one-year snapshot.

What's your investment philosophy?

We believe that investment strategy in retirement must be driven by income needs, not just return targets. The Three Trees structure determines how we invest — each "tree" has a different time horizon and purpose, so each uses different instruments. The Now Tree is in cash and money markets. The Soon Tree uses bonds, bond ladders, and fixed income. The Later Tree uses diversified equities and growth-oriented investments. We are not market-timers or stock-pickers. We build diversified, cost-efficient portfolios aligned with your specific retirement timeline and risk tolerance.

What do you charge? How do you get paid?

We operate as fee-only advisors. We charge an annual advisory fee based on assets under management, typically ranging from 0.75% to 1.0% depending on portfolio size and complexity. We do not earn commissions on product sales, and we do not receive referral fees or kickbacks. Our compensation is simple, transparent, and fully disclosed. The only way we earn more is if your portfolio grows — our incentives are completely aligned with yours.

Do you meet in person or virtually?

Both. We have a physical office and welcome in-person meetings, and we also work with clients across the country virtually via video conference. Many of our best client relationships are fully virtual — technology makes it easy to collaborate on plans, share screens, and review documents in real time from anywhere. We will work in whatever format is most comfortable and convenient for you.

What happens to my money and my plan if something happens to you?

This is an important question and one we take very seriously. Your assets are held at a major independent custodian — not with us personally — so they are always fully accessible and protected. Our affiliation with Altitude Capital Management provides an institutional backbone and continuity plan. Because we are a husband-and-wife partnership, there is always a second advisor who knows your plan intimately. We also have succession agreements in place to ensure your plan and your relationship are never disrupted by the unexpected. Your retirement plan does not depend on any single person.

What do you stand for?

We stand for clarity over complexity. We believe retirement planning has been unnecessarily complicated by an industry that profits from confusion. We stand for fiduciary duty — always putting clients first, never selling products for commissions. We stand for education — we want clients to understand their plan completely, not just trust us blindly. We stand for structure — because a structured plan built on sound principles will always outperform a reactive one built on guesswork. And we stand for the people we serve — the teachers, engineers, business owners, and professionals who have spent decades building something worth protecting.

What happens at a first meeting? How do I get started?

It starts with a free 30-minute discovery call. There's no pitch, no pressure, and no obligation. We'll spend 30 minutes understanding where you are, where you want to go, and whether the Three Trees Framework is the right fit for your retirement. If we both want to move forward, we schedule a comprehensive retirement income analysis — covering your assets, income sources, tax situation, and goals. From there we build your Three Trees plan and present it in a clear, visual format you can actually understand. Ongoing, we meet quarterly to review, rebalance, and adjust as your life evolves. To get started, simply click "Schedule a Free Call" and choose a time that works for you.
