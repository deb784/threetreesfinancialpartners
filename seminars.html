What you’ll learn at this Seminar

Join us for a free educational seminar where you'll learn:

  • When to claim Social Security for maximum lifetime benefit
  • How spousal and survivor benefits work
  • Tax implications of Social Security income
  • Strategies for divorced individuals and widows
  • How to coordinate Social Security with other retirement income

This is a no-cost educational workshop. No products will be sold.

Optimal Filing Age

Discover when to claim Social Security based on your specific situation—and why waiting isn't always the best answer.

Spousal Strategies

Learn how married couples can coordinate benefits to maximize household income over both lifetimes.

Tax Planning

Understand how Social Security income is taxed and strategies to minimize your tax burden in retirement.

Common Mistakes

Avoid the filing errors that cost retirees tens of thousands of dollars.

Who Should Attend?

Who Should Attend?

This seminar is designed for

  • Adults ages 55-75 planning their Social Security filing decision
  • Couples wanting to coordinate spousal benefits
  • Divorced individuals unsure about ex-spouse benefits
  • Widows and widowers navigating survivor benefits
  • Anyone who hasn't filed for Social Security yet

Even if you're already retired, if you haven't filed for Social Security, this seminar can help you make an informed decision.
